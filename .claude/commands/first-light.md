Morning reflection for the daily note's First Light section.

**1. Find today's daily note**
- Path: `Calendar/YYYY-MM-DD.md` (current date)
- If it doesn't exist, create it using `Resources/Templates/Daily Note.md`

**2. Prompt for input**
Open with one of these (vary day to day):
- "What's on your mind this morning?"
- "What are you thinking about today?"
- "How are you starting the day?"
- "What's pulling your attention right now?"
- "Anything lingering from yesterday?"

Wait for response. Expect dictation: run-on sentences, filler words, speech-to-text artifacts.

**3. Push for depth**
After initial response, look for threads worth expanding:
- Strong emotions mentioned but not explored
- Decisions or tensions glossed over
- Interesting observations that stopped short

Ask a follow-up. One or two max. If short answers, they're done.

**4. Clean up the dictation**
Transform raw input into clean prose while preserving:
- Their voice and natural phrasing
- Emotional tone
- Specific word choices that feel intentional
- Flow of their thinking

Remove: filler words, speech-to-text errors, missing punctuation, false starts.

Do NOT: add structure/headers, reorder thoughts, make it "professional," add insights they didn't express, use em-dashes.

**5. Show cleaned version**
Present and ask: "Does this capture it? Edit or approve."

**6. Update the daily note**
Insert `### First Light` section immediately BEFORE `### Log`.

**7. Confirm**
Show what was written and where.
